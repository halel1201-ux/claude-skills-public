# Carousel Types & Templates

## 1. Educational Carousel

**Best for**: Teaching a concept, explaining a process, sharing knowledge
**When to use**: AI tips, landing page how-tos, tech explanations

```
Slide 1  [HOOK]     Bold question or surprising stat
                     "90% of landing pages fail because of ONE thing"

Slide 2  [PROBLEM]  The pain point your audience feels
                     "You spend hours building a page and get zero leads"

Slide 3  [CONTENT]  Step/tip 1 - the foundation
Slide 4  [CONTENT]  Step/tip 2 - building on the first
Slide 5  [CONTENT]  Step/tip 3 - the key insight
Slide 6  [CONTENT]  Step/tip 4 - common mistake to avoid
Slide 7  [CONTENT]  Step/tip 5 - advanced tip
Slide 8  [CONTENT]  Step/tip 6 - the secret weapon

Slide 9  [SUMMARY]  Key takeaway - one sentence recap
                     "The difference between 0 and 100 leads is..."

Slide 10 [CTA]      Clear call to action
                     "Save this for later" / "Follow for more" / "Link in bio"
```

**Visual style**: Clean, minimal, professional. Consistent background with changing foreground elements per step.

## 2. Promotional Carousel

**Best for**: Selling the course, promoting services, launching offers
**When to use**: Course launch, limited offers, testimonials showcase

```
Slide 1  [HOOK]     Result or transformation statement
                     "I built 2 SaaS products without writing a line of code"

Slide 2  [PAIN]     The pain before the solution
                     "I used to pay 15,000 NIS for a single landing page"

Slide 3  [BRIDGE]   "What if I told you..." / the turning point
                     "Then I discovered Claude Code..."

Slide 4  [BENEFIT]  Benefit 1 - the main value
Slide 5  [BENEFIT]  Benefit 2 - speed/ease
Slide 6  [BENEFIT]  Benefit 3 - proof/results
Slide 7  [BENEFIT]  Benefit 4 - unique differentiator

Slide 8  [PROOF]    Social proof - real results, screenshots, numbers
                     "[your live product / client result / real numbers]"

Slide 9  [OFFER]    The offer details
                     "[your price + bonuses]"

Slide 10 [CTA]      Direct CTA
                     "Link in bio" / "DM me 'course'" / "Comment 'yes'"
```

**Visual style**: Premium, aspirational but approachable. Show real products, real screens.

## 3. Tips / Listicle Carousel

**Best for**: Quick value, shareable content, growing reach
**When to use**: Tool lists, mistake lists, hack compilations

```
Slide 1  [HOOK]     Number + topic
                     "7 AI tools that replaced my design team"
                     "5 mistakes killing your landing page"

Slide 2  [TIP 1]    First tip - start with the most surprising/valuable
Slide 3  [TIP 2]    Second tip
Slide 4  [TIP 3]    Third tip
Slide 5  [TIP 4]    Fourth tip
Slide 6  [TIP 5]    Fifth tip
Slide 7  [TIP 6]    Sixth tip (if needed)
Slide 8  [TIP 7]    Seventh tip (if needed)

Slide 9  [BONUS]    Bonus tip or summary
                     "Bonus: The one tool that ties them all together"

Slide 10 [CTA]      Engagement CTA
                     "Which tip was new to you? Comment below"
                     "Save this list for later"
```

**Visual style**: Numbered slides with consistent layout. Bold number on each slide. High contrast, easy to scan.

---

## 4. Storytelling Carousel

**Best for**: Building connection, personal brand, emotional content
**When to use**: Journey stories, failure-to-success, behind-the-scenes

```
Slide 1  [HOOK]     Emotional hook / cliffhanger
                     "I almost gave up on my SaaS last month"
                     "What nobody tells you about building in public"

Slide 2  [SCENE]    Set the scene - where, when, what was happening
                     "3 months ago I was staring at my Stripe dashboard..."

Slide 3  [CONFLICT] The challenge/problem arose
Slide 4  [DEPTH]    It got worse / the real struggle

Slide 5  [TURN]     The turning point / what changed
Slide 6  [ACTION]   What you actually did

Slide 7  [RESULT]   The outcome - with real numbers
Slide 8  [RESULT]   More results / the ripple effect

Slide 9  [LESSON]   The takeaway / what you learned
                     "The lesson: shipping beats perfecting"

Slide 10 [CTA]      Connect to audience's journey
                     "Have you been there? Tell me in the comments"
```

**Visual style**: Personal, warm, behind-the-scenes feel. Photos of real workspace, screens, moments. Less polished, more authentic.

## Type Selection Guide

| If the user's topic is about... | Recommended Type |
|--------------------------------|-----------------|
| How-to, process, explanation   | Educational     |
| Course/product/service         | Promotional     |
| List of tools/tips/mistakes    | Tips/Listicle   |
| Personal experience, journey   | Storytelling    |
| Mixed / unclear                | Ask the user    |

## Hook Formulas (for any type)

| Formula    | Example                                      |
|-----------|----------------------------------------------|
| Question  | "Why do 90% of landing pages fail?"          |
| Bold Stat | "I made $5K/mo with a page I built in 2 hours" |
| Contrarian| "Stop hiring designers for your landing page" |
| Curiosity | "The #1 thing I wish I knew before building my SaaS" |
| Social    | "10,000 people use this method to build sites" |
